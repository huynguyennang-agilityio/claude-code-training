Plan 003: Active tab

Design for the Active page (health gate, Active tab, My Challenges tab) and the one change to the challenge detail (no Pause before the challenge starts). Colours, radii and spacing are the tokens of the app theme (dark theme, Lime). Layout is inspired by the reference screenshots; no artwork or copy from them is used. Frames are 375 wide; the app also supports 430 wide and text scale 2.0.
